VERA is an academic prototype. The agent under test is simulated or a test model, every claim is synthetic, and it is not a Guidewire product. "Certified" means passing VERA's own thresholds, nothing more.

Presenting live: what to click
  1. Start on Overview. Read the sentence, then point at the case lattice: one square per claim.
  2. Click Run assurance suite. With the mock agent it finishes in seconds; a live model takes 4 to 5 minutes.
  3. Click a dark red square. The evidence drawer shows the rule, the values compared and the agent's raw words. Use Next to walk the findings.
  4. Open Batches and Approve or Reject a waiting batch. Type your name; the chip changes and the decision appears in Reviews.
  5. Open Runs to show the run-over-run chart and the drift alerts.
